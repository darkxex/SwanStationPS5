The first release of **SwanStationPS5**, a PlayStation 1 emulator that runs as a native app on a jailbroken PS5. It is a fork of [PSXS5](https://github.com/SynoPiia/PSXS5) that runs every game on [SwanStation](https://github.com/libretro/swanstation) (DuckStation's libretro core), drawn on the PS5's GPU through Vulkan.

## What's new compared with PSXS5

- **SwanStation is the emulator.** The Emulator setting is gone: every game runs on it, at up to 16x the PS1's resolution, with PGXP. PCSX-ReARMed stays in the build only as a fallback for when the screen isn't drawn through Vulkan. Beetle PSX HW is no longer built (`make APP_BEETLE=1` brings it back).
- **SwanStation's own options in the settings menu.** 104 of its core options, in the tab each belongs to: Display, Graphics, Controls (the controller ports) and System (console and advanced). They are saved per game or for every game, and take effect the next time you start a game. They are in English, and only on the console, not on the phone page.
- **Defaults:** the classic digital pad, the BIOS's full start-up (the PlayStation logo; "PS1 startup intro" on) and sharp final scaling.
- **Settings that SwanStation doesn't use are gone:** widescreen from the cheat library, PAL at 60 Hz, anti-aliasing, texture filtering, smooth video colours, deinterlacing, overclock, faster screen effects, dithering, HD texture packs, filter 2D and the built-in HLE BIOS. SwanStation has its own equivalents among its options.
- **Fixes:**
  - Quitting to the shelf no longer closes the app with an error.
  - Changing the language no longer freezes the app.
- **Updates come from this repository's releases.**

## Install

1. Turn on etaHEN and its FTP server (port 2121).
2. Copy the `PPSA97510` folder from the zip to `/data/homebrew/` on the PS5 and give it `777` permissions, recursively. Without them the PS5 says *"Can't start the game or app"*. `tools/psxs5_sync.py app --app-dir PPSA97510 --host <PS5 IP>` does both.
3. Put your games in `/data/PSXS5/games/`.

## BIOS

SwanStation runs without a BIOS file, on its built-in OpenBIOS, which is less compatible. For the best results use a dump of your own console: put it directly in `/data/PSXS5/bios/` as `scph5500.bin` (Japan), `scph5501.bin` (USA) or `scph5502.bin` (Europe). SwanStationPS5 doesn't include one and doesn't download one.

## Known limitations

- SwanStation on the PS5 is new and has had little testing. Tell us which games don't work.
- The shader compile on the first start of a game can freeze the screen for a couple of seconds.
- The new options have no translations yet.
